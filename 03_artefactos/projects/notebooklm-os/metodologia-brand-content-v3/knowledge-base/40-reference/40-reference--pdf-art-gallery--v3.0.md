# Reference roles

Study transformation narratives in S01, rubric and iteration in S04, prototype progression in S08, multimodal hierarchy in S09, orchestration diagrams in S14, and integration/PIVOTE in S15. ART-01–ART-08 may illustrate human-centered technology, information focus, accessible assistance, orchestration, progress, purpose, strategic systems, and thresholds. These are motif labels, not reuse permissions. [METODOLOGIA]

# Study protocol

For each selected source record: dominant idea per page, narrative tension, reading order, information density, visual function, use of whitespace and contrast, transferable rule, historical-only choice, accessibility problem, unsupported claim, and relationship to active canon. Compare at most a small purposeful set; do not ask the notebook to infer style from all 43 PDFs simultaneously.

</knowledge>

# Evidence

The images were extracted exactly from canonical PDFs and are marked `INTERNAL_REFERENCE_ONLY`; their presence does not establish exclusive ownership or public reuse rights. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:ART-GALLERY-V1]

</evidence>
<decisions>

# Decisions

Transfer principles, never trace compositions, characters, or layouts. When a reference conflicts with active visual canon, active canon wins and the difference is treated as historical evolution.

</decisions>
<assumptions>

# Assumptions

[SUPUESTO] The selected page is legible enough to assess its visual function; otherwise record an OCR or rendering gap.

</assumptions>
<limits>

# Limits

References cannot support factual claims, rights, accessibility conformance, or current brand tokens.

</limits>
<edge_cases>

# Edge cases

A beautiful page with poor reading order is a negative fixture. A repeated motif may be corpus habit, not canon. A rasterized page can inspire pacing but not serve as an editable template.

</edge_cases>
<acceptance>

# Acceptance

Reference analysis names the exact source and page, extracts a functional principle, identifies a limitation, checks active canon, and proposes an original application without copying protected expression.

</acceptance>
