# Abstract

The Learn–Appropriate–Evolve model distinguishes encountering and explaining an idea, integrating it into repeatable practice, and revising one's operating model through feedback and transfer. It is a MetodologIA learning reference, not a validated scientific stage theory. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:LEGACY-KB-24-LEARNING-V1-1]

</abstract>

<knowledge>

# Three states

- **Learn:** map vocabulary and relationships, identify sources, explain the concept, and recognize examples and non-examples.
- **Appropriate:** retrieve without scaffolding, adapt the concept to one's workflow, produce evidence, and repeat under relevant variation.
- **Evolve:** test assumptions in a new context, integrate feedback, discard obsolete practices, and version the capability.

Use the states as diagnostic questions: What can the learner explain? What can they perform without rescue? What changes under a new case? What evidence would make them revise the model? [PEDAGOGIA]

</knowledge>
<evidence>

# Evidence

The progression is derived from MetodologIA formation materials. Any claim about memory, cognition, or retention requires separate pedagogical or neuroscience sources. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:LEGACY-KB-24-LEARNING-V1]

</evidence>
<decisions>

# Decisions

Use observable performance and transfer rather than self-reported familiarity to identify a current state.

</decisions>
<assumptions>

# Assumptions

[SUPUESTO] The capability can be practiced and observed in at least one safe context.

</assumptions>
<limits>

# Limits

The states are not necessarily linear, exclusive, or permanent. They do not certify expertise or long-term retention.

</limits>
<edge_cases>

# Edge cases

A learner may appropriate one subskill while only learning another. Prior experience can support transfer without familiarity with MetodologIA vocabulary. Re-label the evidence, not the person.

</edge_cases>
<acceptance>

# Acceptance

Use of the model names the target capability, observable evidence, current provisional state, next practice, feedback source, and limitation; it never assigns status from attendance or confidence alone.

</acceptance>
