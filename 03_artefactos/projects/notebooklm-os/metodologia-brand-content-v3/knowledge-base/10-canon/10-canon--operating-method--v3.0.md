# Abstract

MetodologIA turns intention into verifiable outcomes by designing the way work is understood, executed, checked, and improved. Technology amplifies a governed method; it does not supply purpose, evidence, responsibility, or final approval. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:LEGACY-KB-10-METHOD-V1]

# Core sequence

1. Define purpose, audience, desired outcome, and non-goals.
2. Understand the problem, people, context, constraints, and current evidence.
3. Design or repair the process before choosing automation.
4. Specify success, failure, quality, and human decision points.
5. Execute the smallest useful cycle.
6. Verify the result against evidence and acceptance criteria.
7. Convert repeatable work into a procedure, template, or governed capability.
8. Amplify with technology only where it improves the system.
9. Observe effects, learn, and create a successor version.

Productivity means materializing an intention with appropriate quality and sustainable effort, not maximizing activity. “Impact = intention × time × (method + technology)” is an operating metaphor, not a scientific or financial equation. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:LEGACY-KB-10-METHOD-V1]

# Operating lenses

- **Operator → architect:** move effort upstream into understanding, specification, decisions, and review; delegate bounded mechanics.
- **Presence + automation:** automate repeatable, reversible, observable actions; preserve human presence for ambiguity, trust, originality, sensitive data, irreversible effects, and approval.
- **Zero friction:** remove waiting, rework, context switching, avoidable handoffs, and repeated decisions; retain deliberate friction that protects learning, rights, safety, or quality.
- **Designed excellence:** define a fit-for-purpose rubric, generate, evaluate, correct, and revalidate. Excellence is contextual—not maximal scope.
- **Performance + resilience:** high performance is consistent quality through method and practice; high yield sustains, adapts, and scales that quality without hero dependence.
- **PIVOTE:** People, Interactions, Value, Organization, Technology, Evolution. Historical variants using Time or Effectiveness must be named explicitly and never mixed silently. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:NFC-DRV-S15]

# Evidence status

These definitions are owner-authored methodology synthesized from the formation corpus. They may describe MetodologIA's approach, but they are not universal empirical laws. Product, price, neuroscience, ROI, and causal performance claims require separate current evidence. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:EVIDENCE-CLAIMS-GAPS-V3]

</evidence>
<decisions>

# Decisions

- Prefer a smaller observable cycle over a large speculative build.
- Treat the method, source files, and acceptance criteria as portable authority; treat any AI provider as replaceable infrastructure.
- Create successor versions instead of silently overwriting approved artifacts.

</decisions>

<limits>

# Limits

This canon does not authorize external actions, guarantee impact, prescribe a universal agent count, or replace domain expertise. It cannot turn historical examples into current facts.

</limits>
<edge_cases>

# Edge cases

- If the process is unknown, run discovery; do not automate guesses.
- If efficiency conflicts with safety, rights, or learning, preserve the protective friction.
- If acceptance cannot be observed, label the output `DRAFT` and define the missing test.
- If two PIVOTE variants appear, ask for or declare the selected expansion.

</edge_cases>
<acceptance>

# Acceptance

A method-based answer passes when it states outcome, context, process, criteria, human decisions, evidence status, limits, and next gate; it fails when it equates speed or volume with value.

</acceptance>
