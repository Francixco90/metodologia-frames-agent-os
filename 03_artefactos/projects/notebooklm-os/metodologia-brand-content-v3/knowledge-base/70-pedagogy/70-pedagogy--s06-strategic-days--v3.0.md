# Abstract

S06 converts reclaimed time into future capacity by aligning agenda, attention, decisions, learning, and review with an intended outcome. A full calendar is evidence of allocation, not proof of strategy. [METODOLOGIA][source_ref:NFC-DRV-S06]

</abstract>

# Routing

Use for S06 and calendar redesign. Calendar mutations require separate authorization; health or therapy questions route outside this curriculum.

</routing>
<knowledge>

# Prerequisites and concepts

Prerequisite: one real week of calendar/task evidence with sensitive details removed. Concepts: outcome versus urgency; fragmentation; invisible work; focus, maintenance, learning, recovery, and contingency blocks; time reinvestment; reflective questions versus clinical advice.

# Sequence

Read the week as evidence. Map blocks to outcomes. Identify fragmentation and recurring delegation candidates. Design a successor week with protected focus, maintenance, learning, and review. Use one reflective question at a time to expose assumptions. End by deciding where reclaimed time will go.

# Misconceptions

A packed agenda is strategic; automation should precede measurement; AI coaching is therapy; reclaimed time should automatically accept more work; product names and salary examples remain current.

# Practice and transfer

Create a before/after calendar plan with rationale, dependencies, interruption rule, review point, and intended evidence. Do not import events without a separate gate. [PEDAGOGIA]

</knowledge>
<evidence>

# Evidence

Expected evidence: allocation audit, stated outcomes, protected blocks, explicit trade-offs, contingency, and end-of-week readback.

</evidence>
<decisions>

# Decisions

Protect capacity for review and learning rather than filling all availability. Treat reflective AI output as prompts for judgment, not diagnosis.

</decisions>
<assumptions>

# Assumptions

[SUPUESTO] The learner controls at least part of the schedule or can negotiate constraints.

</assumptions>
<limits>

# Limits

The lesson does not diagnose burnout, prescribe treatment, or guarantee recovered time. Calendar screenshots must not expose PII.

</limits>
<edge_cases>

# Edge cases

If the learner has little calendar control, redesign decision rules and escalation instead of blocks. If interruptions are intrinsic, use buffers and service-level expectations.

</edge_cases>
<acceptance>

# Acceptance

The learner explains how the planned day serves outcomes, preserves review and contingency, names one trade-off, and defines evidence for the next weekly adjustment. [PEDAGOGIA]

</acceptance>
