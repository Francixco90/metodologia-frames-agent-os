# Masterclass PDF operating contract

[METODOLOGIA] Accept only a strict, hash-bound `trainer-masterclass-content-v1` whose route and
design bindings match the run. Resolve requested `dist/masterclass/{locale}/masterclass.pdf`
outputs from the artifact plan. The compiler—not this skill—is the implementation authority.

Require three rights-cleared local authority receipts: browser policy `none-native-pdf`, exact
`process.version`, and Helvetica PDF Standard-14. Reject missing, reordered, stale or unlisted
receipts. Resolve theme values from the locked token projection; never substitute arbitrary
colors or fonts.

[PEDAGOGIA] Require exactly 18 ordered moments. Base minutes total 90; optional extension totals
30. Materialize the shared lede and each moment body rather than treating titles as the lesson.

Emit tagged PDF 1.4 with 18 pages, language metadata, one H1 and three P structures tied to
distinct MCIDs on every page. Mark backgrounds and geometric accents as `Artifact`. Preserve
selectable reading order and localized base/extension timing. Reject unsupported WinAnsi
glyphs and text that exceeds deterministic line budgets.

Compile twice in separate processes. Compare official bytes, manifests, receipts and exact
output tree. Reject timestamps, randomness, network, symlinks, pre-existing staging, mutations,
undeclared outputs, locators and PII. An HTML object viewer may aid local review but has no
publication authority and must constrain its locale and PDF reference.

[INFERENCIA] Tagged structure and static contrast checks do not replace visual, accessible or
pedagogical review. Preserve `coverage_gap` until independent verification.

[SUPUESTO] Only local synthetic fixtures are eligible while lifecycle is `candidate`.
