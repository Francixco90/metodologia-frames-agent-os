# Brand knowledge pack

## Active layers

- `00 Control`: knowledge map, authority, routing, gates, injection defense and response locale.
- `10 Canon`: confirmed identity, positioning, audience, voice, rhetoric and visual system.
- `20 Evidence`: approved claims, provenance, freshness, contradictions and gaps.
- `30 Templates`: channel or Studio contracts; never evidence for claims.
- `40 Golden References`: at most two per channel, annotated by the behavior worth emulating.
- `50 Assets`: stable asset IDs, digests, variants, rights, permitted and forbidden uses.
- `60 Operations`: naming, source selection, receipts, feedback and successor policy.

Archive is excluded from default source sets. Do not keep competing active versions.

## Source-set design

Create named, bounded sets for identity, voice, channel, claims and assets. Chat should generally use
3–8 sources and Studio 4–12. Selecting every source is blocked for brand generation because it makes
authority and repeatability opaque.

## Acceptance

The pack is ready for review when its brand, owner, audience, locale, authority order, rights states,
source sets and approver are explicit. Activation additionally requires no competing canon, no
blocked item in an active set and approval bound to the pack digest.
