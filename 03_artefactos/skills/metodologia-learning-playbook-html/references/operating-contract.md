# Playbook operating contract

[METODOLOGIA] Accept only hash-bound `trainer-extended-content-v1` whose route and design locks
match the run. Resolve `dist/playbook/{locale}/index.html` from the artifact plan. Invoke the
shared extended adapter; this skill is routing guidance, not an implementation surface.

[PEDAGOGIA] Require 12 essential chapters. Permit at most seven optional chapters, each declared
in content, then require `materializedContentIds` to equal that complete set. Omit material that does
not strengthen evidence or transfer. Require purpose and at least one observable step per
chapter; preserve unique IDs and order across locales.

Keep all content in semantic initial HTML. Provide fragment navigation, localized CTA of one to
three words, local styles and clean printing. Keep core content and navigation usable without JS;
copy remains an optional enhancement. Rebuild in isolated roots and
compare exact bytes, manifest, receipt and output tree. Reject plan/content Cartesian drift,
undeclared optionals, duplicate IDs, missing steps, remote assets, tracking, response persistence,
private locators, symlinks, stale hashes and publication.

[INFERENCIA] Static conformance does not prove pedagogical or visual quality. Preserve
`coverage_gap` pending independent evaluation. [SUPUESTO] Use synthetic fixtures only while
candidate. [NEUROCIENCIA] Reject unsupported cognitive claims. `coverage_gap`: arbitrary subset
selection and cross-locale structural parity need dedicated adapter-contract changes.
