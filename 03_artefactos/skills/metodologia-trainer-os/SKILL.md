# Trainer OS

Route a topic and audience into governed training artifacts.

1. Normalize without inventing evidence. Ask at most two blocking questions.
2. Present exactly two distinct directions with assumptions and trade-offs.
3. Lock route and design specifications after selection.
4. Route declared artifacts to registered capabilities. Never author HTML or PDF here.
5. Verify hashes, replay, privacy and acceptance. Stop at `RENDERED_DRAFT`.

Use four prompts: brief, direction, review, close. Allow three when no decision remains and five
for one material gap; then record `coverage_gap`. Apply progressive disclosure: load
[references/operating-contract.md](references/operating-contract.md) only for contract detail.
Keep real content, receipts and private locators outside Frames. Never publish. Runtime remains
blocked pending evaluation.
